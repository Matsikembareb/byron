When I joined Powervision as an intern I expected to observe.

Instead I was handed ownership of tasks that forced me to grow: normalizing schemas, implementing RBAC in Flask/SQLAlchemy, writing migrations and tests, and following professional dev workflows. Suddenly the courses I once treated as abstract — database design, software development processes, testing, ethics, and professionalism — are all making sense and coming together in real work.

**The biggest lesson:** theory matters, but discipline, testing, clear processes, and respectful communication are what make software reliable and teams stronger.

I know there are brothers and sisters out there struggling to find their breakthrough, gain skill, or find anywhere they fit in. This is a good time — you’ve got a whole year to learn, and if you’re lucky, on your terms too. My wake-up call to take my future into my own hands was getting hired; this post might be yours. Believe in yourself and give it time.

I’m grateful for the opportunity and for a team that pushes me toward craftsmanship and responsible engineering.
